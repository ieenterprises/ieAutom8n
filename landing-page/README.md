# ieAutom8n Landing Page

Official marketing landing page for **ieAutom8n** by **ieEnterprises**.

## Features Included
- **Modern Dark/Neon UI:** Styled with deep slate and royal purple glows (`#8B5CF6`).
- **Hero & Live Canvas Visual:** Interactive diagram showcasing automated webhook-to-AI-agent pipelines.
- **Direct App Integration:** All "Sign In" and "Launch App" buttons route directly to your live app: `https://ieautom8n-latest.onrender.com/signin`.
- **Responsive:** Works seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and widescreen monitors.
- **Business Contact:** Inquiries link directly to `ieenterprises.ai@gmail.com`.

## Free 1-Minute Deployment on Render

You can host this landing page for free on Render as a **Static Site**:
1. In your [Render Dashboard](https://dashboard.render.com), click **+ New** $\rightarrow$ **Static Site**.
2. Connect your repository: `https://github.com/ieenterprises/ieAutom8n`.
3. In the setup fields:
   - **Name:** `ieautom8n` (or `ieenterprises`)
   - **Branch:** `main`
   - **Publish Directory:** `landing-page`
4. Click **Create Static Site**.

Render will deploy your landing page instantly with a free SSL certificate!
You can also attach your custom root domain (like `ieenterprises.ai` or `ieautom8n.com`) in Render's settings.
